About This Course

This comprehensive Machine Learning course provides a structured foundation in supervised and unsupervised learning techniques. Students will work with regression, classification, neural networks, model evaluation strategies and production-level deployment workflows.

By the end of this program, you will be capable of building, evaluating and deploying ML models in real-world environments.

Course Syllabus

  • Week 1: Introduction to Machine Learning
  • Week 2: Linear & Logistic Regression
  • Week 3: Decision Trees & Random Forest
  • Week 4: Support Vector Machines
  • Week 5: Neural Networks
  • Week 6-8: Advanced ML Algorithms
  • Week 9-10: Model Optimization
  • Week 11-12: Capstone Project
